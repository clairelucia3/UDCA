Awards

The Awards program recognizes individuals who demonstrate outstanding dedication, leadership, and service within the community. Through these honors, the association celebrates students, educators, and volunteers who make meaningful contributions and embody the values of excellence, initiative, and community impact.

UDCA Award

The UDCA Medal Program was established to encourage students within the UDCA community to strive for excellence in academics, extracurricular activities, leadership, and community service. By recognizing outstanding achievement in these areas, the program seeks to uphold high expectations and promote well-rounded education and excellence among all students.

Application Deadline: August 31 of each year. The award ceremony is to be held during the annual UDCA Thanksgiving Families and Friends Night

UDHS Senior Award

From 2016 onward, UDCA-sponsored scholarships have been awarded annually to two outstanding UDHS graduating seniors who have qualified as National Merit Finalists while maintaining excellent extracurricular performance records. This award is nominated by UDHS faculty. UDCA is the sponsor only.

Inspire Award

The INSPIRE award is sponsored by the Upper Dublin Chinese American Association and is awarded to an individual who consistently demonstrates the characteristics of an excellent educator, which include being Innovative, Nurturing, Spirited, Passionate, Inclusive, Resilient, and Empowering. The Recipient is announced at the annual Lunar New Year Gala.
